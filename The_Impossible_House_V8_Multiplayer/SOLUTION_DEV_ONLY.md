## V7 required brass orrery (Chapter 3)

Once a player reaches the Crossing Room, set the three windows to Star, Clock, Moon (left to right). Read all three lines of the inscription for the clues. The exit remains locked until aligned.

## V7 optional hunts

Chapters 2 and 6 include optional 75-second hunts with five terms each. These do not count toward the story exit. Their layouts are deterministic; follow the first/last letter instructions and play with another authenticated browser to verify real-time updates.
