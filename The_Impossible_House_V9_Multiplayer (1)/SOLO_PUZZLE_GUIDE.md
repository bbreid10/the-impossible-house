# Solo Playtest: How to Start (No Story Spoilers)

Open `PLAY_SOLO_OFFLINE.html`, or the separate `The_Impossible_House_V7_Solo.html` download. Choose **Try a 60-second practice**, **Start the mystery** or **Guided beginner mode**, or **Choose a chapter** to start any of the six puzzles directly.

You control two explorers in solo mode. Tap their portraits near the top to switch. The inactive explorer stays in place. Multiplayer mode assigns one explorer to each friend's device instead.

## The four core interactions

1. **Walk:** Gold is your current room. Click a neighboring green-outlined room to walk there. Two facing doors must connect.
2. **Turn:** Use the ↶ and ↷ buttons on a glowing cog room. Some need a switch first. Turning one may move another gear room if the Linked Rooms card is active.
3. **Rewrite:** Find word fragments, inscribe a magical sentence, then cast its Word Rule. Only one special rule can be active at once.
4. **Coordinate:** Different explorers must occupy the two seals simultaneously to open the exit. Then bring everyone to the exit room.

## New thinking challenges

- **Chapter 4, Portrait Parliament:** Each explorer starts by a note. Read both notes, enter the Judgment Hall, and choose the symbol that fits both statements. Wrong choices are safe.
- **Chapter 5, Borrowed Hour:** Read the notes in both starting rooms, cast Linked Rooms to help the explorer by the observatory, and activate the three runes in the logical order. Wrong orders reset only the rune progress.

The optional **Guided** mode shows useful moves. Choose Mischief Challenge to try the house without the full step-by-step guide. Nobody loses lives, and Story Mode has no countdown.

**The downloaded HTML is not online multiplayer.** Run the server or deploy it publicly using `DEPLOY_ON_RENDER.md` to let friends on different devices join.
