## Competition scoring | Four equally weighted dimensions

- **Execution 25%:** Server-authoritative game logic, synchronized game state, join codes, private/public rooms, reconnect flow, six stages, 17 passing automated tests. Risks: in-memory room storage and free-hosting sleep/restarts.
- **Creativity 25%:** Rotating physical architecture plus magical word-rule cards plus two-player clue and rune puzzles. Risk: card construction is still mostly prescribed in the first three stages.
- **Value 25%:** Short, replayable co-op experience, optional solo testing, no punitive time limits, cosmetics, saves, and selectable chapters. Need unbiased player feedback to confirm replay appeal.
- **Polish 25%:** Hand-drawn SVG room art, customization, desktop/mobile layout, dynamic board indications, tutorial, hints and contextual controls. Need novice usability and accessibility testing.
